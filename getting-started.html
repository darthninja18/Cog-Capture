Getting Started

Cog & Capture is built around Pixelmon, Create, exploration and building up your world over time.

This guide is being written. We will document the actual pack progression here rather than guessing at mechanics.

Early priorities

Food can take a little more effort to establish than in a normal Minecraft world. Farming and Farmer's Delight are useful early investments.

Explore and build

Exploration matters in Cog & Capture. As you establish a base, Create becomes a major part of building and automation while Pixelmon remains tied into the world around you.
