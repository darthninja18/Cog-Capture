Pasture

The Pasture gives Pokémon a useful role around your base instead of leaving every resource to vanilla mobs.

Example: eggs

Bird Pokémon can provide a renewable route to eggs through the Pasture. This gives eggs a Pixelmon-focused source while keeping normal eggs useful in Minecraft recipes.

Exact Pokémon, inputs, outputs and rates will be documented here as they are verified in the current release.
