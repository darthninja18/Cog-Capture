CUSTOM INTEGRATION

Pixelmon XP Bridge

Pixelmon replaces much of Minecraft's ordinary hostile-mob combat. XP Bridge makes Pokémon battles contribute to normal Minecraft experience without replacing Pixelmon's own Pokémon XP.

Current behavior

Defeating wild Pokémon awards Minecraft XP, trainer battles receive increased Minecraft XP rewards, and PvP battles do not add Minecraft XP. Mining, smelting and other normal Minecraft XP sources remain unchanged.

This exists so enchanting and other vanilla XP systems still fit naturally into a Pixelmon-centered survival world.
