Tips & Tricks

Useful things that are easy to miss when Pixelmon, Create and Cog & Capture's custom systems start overlapping.

🎒 Upgrade your backpack for battles

The Battle Upgrade lets supported battle items in a Sophisticated Backpack appear in Pixelmon battles. Keep your Poké Balls and healing supplies together instead of constantly moving stacks to your inventory.

⚙ Exploration feeds engineering

Don't treat exploration as something you finish before building a factory. Pokémon encounters and world resources are intentionally part of progression.

⛏ GeOre replaced the old crystal route

Modern Cog & Capture releases use the GeOre-based renewable resource system. Old Budding Crystals setups should not be used as a current progression guide.

🍳 Establish food early

Traditional livestock is less central in a Pixelmon world. Farming and Farmer's Delight are useful early, while Pasture provides additional Pokémon-centered resource routes.

✨ Pokémon battles contribute to Minecraft XP

The Cog & Capture XP Bridge connects battling to normal Minecraft experience progression, so enchanting does not depend entirely on traditional hostile-mob combat.

🧭 Keep exploring

Important Pixelmon resources such as evolution stones are intentionally not turned into trivial factory products. Your factory supports exploration rather than replacing it.
