CUSTOM INTEGRATION

Pixelmon Battle Upgrade

The Pixelmon Battle Upgrade connects Pixelmon battles with Sophisticated Backpacks. Install the upgrade in a backpack and supported battle items stored inside it become available from Pixelmon's battle menus.

What it does

  • Lets Poké Balls stored in the backpack be selected and thrown during battle.
  • Lets supported healing and other battle items be used directly from the backpack.
  • Consumes the item from the real backpack stack when it is used.
  • Removing the upgrade removes access to those backpack items in battle.

Compatibility

The upgrade was introduced into Cog & Capture 1.5 on the Pixelmon 9.3.16 line. A later 0.1.5 compatibility build was tested successfully with Pixelmon 9.4.1.

Why it exists: A backpack should be useful as storage without forcing players to move every Poké Ball or potion back into their normal inventory before a battle.
