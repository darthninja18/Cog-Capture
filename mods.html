PACK REFERENCE

Mods & Integrations

Cog & Capture is built for Minecraft 1.21.1 on NeoForge. The public CurseForge page identifies Pixelmon and Create as the core gameplay pairing, with Applied Energistics 2, Farmer's Delight and custom compatibility work supporting the pack.

Core gameplay

PixelmonPokémon exploration, battling and the adventure side of the pack.
CreateMechanical engineering, factories, trains and automation.
Applied Energistics 2Advanced storage and logistics.
Farmer's DelightExpanded farming and cooking in a world with less emphasis on vanilla livestock.

Verified additions from published releases

Published Cog & Capture release notes also verify Applied Flux, Flux Networks, Ender Storage, Create: Solar Powered, Glodium, CodeChicken Lib, Explorer's Compass, Nature's Compass, Inventory Trash Cans, GeOre integration and the pack's custom additions.

Cog & Capture custom work

Pixelmon Battle UpgradeBattle directly from a properly upgraded Sophisticated Backpack.Pixelmon XP BridgeEarn normal Minecraft XP through Pokémon battles.Pixelmon PasturePokémon-centered renewable resources around your base.Recipe & GeOre IntegrationCompatibility changes that connect the pack's progression systems.
