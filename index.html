Welcome to Cog & Capture

Cog & Capture combines Pixelmon and Create with supporting mods and custom compatibility work. The goal isn't simply to put both mods in the same pack. Systems are adjusted so exploration, Pokémon, automation and progression connect naturally.

The idea behind the pack

Pixelmon gives you a reason to explore. Create gives you a reason to build. Cog & Capture is about connecting those two loops instead of letting them exist beside each other.
