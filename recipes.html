Recipe Changes

Cog & Capture changes selected recipes when doing so helps Pixelmon, Create and the rest of the pack work together.

Current behavior: Pixelmon berries are no longer used as substitutes for eggs. Recipes use their intended egg ingredients again, with eggs obtainable through the pack's current gameplay systems.
